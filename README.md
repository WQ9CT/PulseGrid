**PulseGrid** is a modern, high-performance web-based reflex and focus trainer. 

Inspired by the classic **Schulte Grid**—a proven psychological method for improving peripheral vision and speed reading—PulseGrid brings this powerful brain-training exercise into the digital age with a sleek UI, smooth animations, and intense challenge modes.

---

## 🧠 What is the Schulte Grid?

The **Schulte Grid** was originally developed by German psychiatrist and psychotherapist Walter Schulte to study the properties of attention. 

By regularly practicing with PulseGrid, you can achieve several cognitive benefits:
- **Enhanced Visual Focus:** Train your eyes to quickly locate specific targets in a complex visual field.
- **Improved Speed Reading:** Expand your peripheral vision, allowing you to process larger blocks of text at a single glance.
- **Faster Reaction Times:** Sharpen your brain-to-hand coordination and reflexes.
- **Deep Concentration:** A perfect 5-minute warm-up exercise before studying, competitive esports gaming, or deep mental work.

## ✨ Features

### 🎛️ Customizable Grid Sizes
Whether you are a beginner or a grandmaster, you can seamlessly scale the difficulty. Use the smooth slider to set your grid anywhere from **5x5 (25 cells)** up to a mind-bending **10x10 (100 cells)**.

### 🔥 Extreme Mode (Stackable Modifiers)
Want to push your limits? Enable **Extreme Mode** and stack any combination of these hardcore modifiers:
- 👁️ **Hidden:** Every cell stays covered. Tap the eye above the grid to peek at all numbers for 1 second—you only get limited peeks based on your grid size.
- 💀 **NoFail:** One wrong tap ends the run immediately. Precision becomes just as important as speed.
- ⏱️ **Clock:** A strict 5-second countdown runs alongside your timer. Correct taps add 2s, wrong taps cost 1s. If the clock hits zero, your run is over.

### 📸 Share Your Achievements
Once you clear a grid, PulseGrid generates a beautiful, stylized **Results Card** image directly in your browser. With one click, you can download or share your final time, mistakes, and active mods with your friends to challenge them!
